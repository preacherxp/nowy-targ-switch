# Nowy Targ scene references

Researched 10 November 2026 against city, cultural-centre, aeroclub, heritage and transport sources, and dated photographs. No reference material was supplied, so everything here was gathered online. This is a contemporary **compressed panorama**, not one street view. The town hall, the lime trees, the shrine, the café pavilion and the tenements all stand on the Rynek, but not in this order; St Catherine's tower stands about 150 m north-west of the square; and the Tatra range lies to the south, while the photographs suggest the town hall's front also faces south. The picture turns the town hall round so that the mountains stand behind it, as they do behind the church towers in the long-lens photographs every resident knows. The season is mid-August, on the days of the Jarmark Podhalański (14–16 August 2026), when stalls and highlander carts are on the square.

## The ordinary

- **Paving:** the square is laid with large pale grey granite slabs in a grid, divided by narrow bands of darker small setts (2015 and 2020 photographs). The streets round it are cobbled with small grey setts (2021 and 2026 photographs).
- **Lamps:** black posts with two arms, each carrying a square glass lantern, and a basket of pink petunias below (2015).
- **Furniture:** square pale concrete planters with dark purple grasses; benches of orange-brown slats on grey frames; tall pink flower towers; white square café parasols; three white flagpoles.
- **Trees:** two big limes in front of the town hall, a tall spruce to its left, clipped small trees along the frontages.
- **Fountain:** jets rising straight from the paving in front of the town hall, lit at night.
- **Traffic:** cars park along the north and south frontages; yellow MZK buses pass along the cobbled streets.
- **Season and weather:** mid-August. The range is bare rock in summer, with only a few snow patches. Lens-shaped clouds over the ridge are the sign of the halny wind.

## Composition recommendation

These are design suggestions in logical pixels, not geographical claims.

- **Skyline:** the Tatra ridge runs across the whole width, peaks between y = 44 and 62, over a forest band at y = 78–92. St Catherine's tower rises behind the left tenements at x ≈ 36–50, cross near y = 16. The town hall stands in the middle, x = 122–198, eaves at y = 78, the spire reaching y ≈ 20 at x = 160. Limes flank it at x ≈ 110 and 212. Tenements close both sides with eaves near y = 84–88.
- **Square:** the shrine at x ≈ 68–80 before the orange house; stalls at the left, x = −14 to 62; the pavilion at x = 230–284; a sheepskin stall beyond it.
- **Lettering:** `MUZEUM` in the 3-pixel font on the balcony, 29 pixels wide at x ≈ 146–174, y ≈ 96–102. `OSCYPKI` in the 5-pixel font on the stall's fascia, 43 pixels wide, at x ≈ 18–60, y ≈ 99–107.
- **Ground:** building feet at y = 118; slabs to y = 130; kerb at y = 131–132; the cobbled street below, wheels at y = 145.
- **Life:** people on foot at y = 123–127; fountain jets in front of the door; a fiddle trio; a queue at the cheese stall; pigeons on the pavilion roof. On the street, in turn: a highlander cart with two horses, the yellow bus, two touring cyclists. In the sky: the tow plane and glider by day, a balloon in the evening.
- **Sky:** sun high to the left of the spire at noon, setting behind the rounded western end of the range at the right, rising over the jagged eastern end at the left.

## As built

The scene follows the layout above, with these changes made after looking at the previews and the real switch:

- The ridge was raised seven rows (peaks at y = 37–55) so that it reads over the roofs, and plain lower houses were added in the two gaps beside the town hall, where bare background showed.
- `MUZEUM` is drawn as dark letters on a pale lit board, 29 × 7 at x = 146–174, y = 95–101, after the oval sign in the 2020 photograph; a dark board was too heavy for the façade. The spruce moved three pixels left to clear `OSCYPKI`.
- The street is shared on a 104-second round: the cart (40 pixels long, 11.5 px/s), then the bus (58 pixels, 25 px/s, right to left), then the two cyclists. The cart runs until about 19:30 and from 8:00, the bus until 22:30 and from 5:30, the cyclists until 20:30 and from 6:30. Each is sent out only between rounds, so one caught by nightfall finishes its crossing: the cart then carries a lantern and the tow plane a red light. Vehicle tops (y = 130) stay two rows clear of the lowest walking lane (feet at y = 127).
- A zebra crossing was added on the cobbles after the 2021 bus photograph. No parked cars are drawn.
- The pigeons sit on the pavilion roof, as in the December 2020 photograph, and fly one loop when the cart passes or the lens is flicked.
- The fiddle trio, the bench sitters, the stall customers, the flag (white and red, on the middle pole; the poles are bare in the photographs) and the hours of every light are invented everyday detail. The fountain's wave is a guess at its programme; it is switched off from midnight to seven.
- Both clocks show the hour. The museum's windows go dark soon after closing; the tower lantern, the floodlights, the lamps and the pavilion follow separate schedules.
- The lens clouds and the single evening balloon are artistic choices.

## Considered and not drawn

- **Kościół św. Anny:** the wooden cemetery church is on a hill outside the centre, among trees; its exterior photograph shows a dark shingle roof with two small onion turrets. St Catherine's is the tower residents see over the roofs, so St Anne's is left out rather than squeezed in.
- **Miejska Hala Lodowa, ul. Parkowa 14, and Podhale Nowy Targ:** the club plays in the top league in 2026/27, but only an interior photograph of the hall was opened, so the building is not drawn.
- **Dunajec and Velo Dunajec:** the rivers meet east of the centre and the cycle route follows them; the two cyclists stand for it, the water is not shown.
- **Kościół Najświętszego Serca Pana Jezusa:** the red brick tower appears in the long-lens photographs but was not researched on its own.
- **Stage of the Jarmark:** seen in the gallery, left out to keep the town hall clear.

## Limits

No visit was made and no photograph was commissioned. Which way the town hall's front faces was not found in a written source; it is inferred from photographs. The newest photographs opened of the town hall are from July 2020, of the pavilion from December 2020, of the shrine from July 2020 and of the bus from July 2021; whether the MUZEUM sign, the planters and the benches are unchanged in 2026 was not verified. The mountain profile follows the March 2019 photograph loosely and no single peak is claimed. Sizes of the church tower and the range against the town hall are a visual choice, enlarged as a long lens does. Oscypek and sheepskins are verified for the weekly market at Nowa Targowica and regional food for the August fair; a stall lettered OSCYPKI on the Rynek is a stand-in for both. Night lighting colours and schedules are artistic, guided by the April 2015 night photograph. The Fotopolska gallery and the live webcam were not opened.
